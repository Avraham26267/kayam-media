Kayam Posts – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Kayam Posts is an internal tool used only by the Kayam team (kayamfit.com) to prepare photo carousels and send them as drafts to the team’s own TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Use

Only accounts owned by the Kayam team may connect to Kayam Posts. Content is sent to the TikTok inbox as a draft; a team member reviews, edits and publishes it manually in the TikTok app.

Data

Kayam Posts stores only the access tokens needed to send drafts to the team’s accounts. It does not collect data from other TikTok users. See our Privacy Policy.

Content

All content is created by the Kayam team and must follow TikTok’s Community Guidelines. AI-generated images are labeled as such when published.

Contact

Questions: through the contact details on kayamfit.com.
