Kayam Posts – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Kayam Posts is an internal tool of the Kayam team (kayamfit.com). It only connects to TikTok accounts owned by the Kayam team, to send our own photo carousels as drafts.

What we access

Basic profile information of the team’s own TikTok accounts (user.info.basic) and permission to upload drafts (video.upload). Nothing else.

What we store

Only the access tokens needed to send drafts, stored privately on our server. We do not collect, sell or share any data about other TikTok users.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); we then delete the stored tokens.

For the Kayam app itself, see kayamfit.com/privacy.html.
